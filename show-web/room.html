How to move & play

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk. Drag the scene to look. Phone: use the movement stick and drag to look. Invited performers can drag knobs, press switches and connect cable ports directly.

VR: thumbstick to walk; grip + stick to rise or turn. B/Y opens your room panel. Grip + B/Y brings it in front of you.

Press H to hide or restore the interface. Escape closes this panel.
